# 2. What the reviews have already established

Do not spend iteration 01 rediscovering these results.

## 2.1 Exact repository-convention topological derivative

For the current lossless, nonmagnetic TMz dielectric problem and the production
objective

\[
J=
\frac12\sum_f\frac{w_f}{s_f^2}
\sum_s |p_{sf}-d_{sf}|^2,
\]

the current-domain topological derivative for inserting the same interior
material at exterior point \(z\) is

\[
\boxed{
D_TJ(z;\Omega)
=
\sum_f\frac{w_f}{s_f^2}
\operatorname{Re}\left[
(k_i^2-k_e^2)
\sum_s
\overline{(p_{sf}-d_{sf})}\,
u_{sf}(z)\,
G_f(z,x_r(s))
\right].
}
\]

Here:

- \(u_{sf}(z)\) is the current-domain total field generated by the physical
  source and therefore already contains the physical source strength;
- \(G_f(z,x_r(s))\) is the current-domain total field at \(z\) for a **unit**
  line source at the paired receiver;
- the measurement sum is over the **declared observed paired entries only**;
- there is **no leading minus sign**;
- there is **no factor \(1/2\)** in the final derivative.

The oracle review checked

\[
Q_\rho(z)
=
\frac{J(\Omega\cup B_\rho(z))-J(\Omega)}
{\pi\rho^2}
\to D_TJ(z;\Omega)
\]

and obtained about `0.066%` relative error at the strong 0.5-GHz probe for
\(\rho=0.625\) mm, with the expected convergence and correct sign at favourable
and unfavourable locations.

Therefore **G1 is now an implementation regression gate, not a derivation
research task**.

## 2.2 Frequency is a topology-discovery regime variable

On the declared two-circle scene, the saved oracle review found:

- `0.25 GHz`: localization error about `1.7 mm`;
- `0.50 GHz`: localization error about `3.3 mm`;
- `0.75 GHz`: localization error about `1.7 mm`;
- `1.0–2.0 GHz`: the TD may localize far from the missing component;
- at `1.5 GHz`, \(D_TJ\) is positive at the true missing centre and an
  infinitesimal insertion there increases the objective;
- `2.5 GHz` happens to localize the centre again, demonstrating that the
  dependence is non-monotone.

For the 35-mm target in `epsr=6` sand, the reliable low band corresponds roughly
to

\[
k_e r_B \lesssim 1.4.
\]

Iteration 01 therefore **freezes 0.50 GHz** as the topology-discovery/training
frequency:

\[
k_e r_B \approx 0.898.
\]

Do not tune this during execution.

The high-frequency failure is retained as an important limitation of the
small-inclusion TD regime. Do not try to rescue it with threshold or
normalization tuning in this iteration.

## 2.3 The inspection region must stay away from the acquisition ring

The raw TD field is singular at sources and receivers. The saved review showed
that a square extending onto the acquisition ring can make the global minimum a
station singularity and corrupt the relative threshold.

Freeze the TD inspection region to

\[
\boxed{
\|x-(0.50,0.50)\|\le 0.20\ {\rm m}.
}
\]

The acquisition ring has radius `0.30 m`, so every inspection point is at least
`0.10 m` from every station before other masks are applied.

## 2.4 Objective decrease alone does not certify a correct birth

At 1.5 GHz the review produced an objective-decreasing birth about `66 mm` away
from the true missing centre. Therefore topology qualification must report:

- localization error;
- number of threshold regions;
- actual objective decrease.

The first two are **evaluation-only** diagnostics. They must never be fed back
into the optimizer, threshold, seed or line search.

# 3. Literature practice retained in the implementation

The implementation should remain recognizable as the mature
topological-derivative + star-shaped-parametric inverse pattern described by
Carpio, Dimiduk, Le Louër and Rapún and by the iterative topological-sensitivity
literature.

Preserve these principles:

1. **Topology and shape are different updates.**  
   The radial optimizer handles smooth shape refinement for fixed \(M\).
   \(D_TJ\) proposes a discrete change in \(M\).

2. **Use the TD of the current approximate domain.**  
   T1 and later use \(D_TJ(z;\Omega_k)\), not an empty-background imaging
   formula.

3. **Threshold the favourable TD region and label connected components.**  
   A relative threshold provides a topology proposal, not a direct estimate of
   the final object boundary.

4. **Use a simple star-shaped seed, then refine parametrically.**  
   The first implementation uses a circular radial-Fourier seed.

5. **Score every finite topology proposal with the actual forward objective.**  
   The asymptotic derivative never commits geometry by itself.

6. **Restart the smooth optimizer after a topology event.**  
   Parameter dimension, Jacobian and damping state are rebuilt.

7. **Do not interpret the equivalent-area radius as recovered physical size.**  
   The saved review already showed that it varies strongly with frequency and
   threshold while the centroid remains stable.

8. **Stay inside a regime where the small-inclusion TD is informative.**  
   Frequency continuation/topology scheduling is a future research problem;
   iteration 01 proves the low-band mechanism first.

Primary literature anchors remain those already indexed in the initial brief:

- Carpio, Dimiduk, Le Louër, Rapún, *JCP* 2019,
  `10.1016/j.jcp.2019.03.027`;
- Le Louër & Rapún, *SIAM J. Imaging Sciences* 2018,
  iterative current-domain topological sensitivity;
- Le Louër & Rapún, *SIAM J. Imaging Sciences* 2017,
  electromagnetic topological sensitivity;
- Carpio, Pena & Rapún, *Inverse Problems* 2021,
  2-D/3-D Fresnel data;
- Carpio & Rapún, *Symmetry* 2021,
  multifrequency topological derivative practice.

# 5. Software architecture

## 5.1 New multi-radial state

Add a small immutable state, for example:

```text
MultiRadialFourierState
    components: tuple[RadialFourierCurveState, ...]
```

Required contract:

- at least one component;
- unique existing `component_id`s;
- stored component order is authoritative;
- deterministic flatten/unflatten parameter slices;
- no secondary ID registry;
- component IDs persist across every fixed-topology step;
- an accepted birth appends a new component ID, e.g. `td_birth_001`;
- each curve is built with the existing
  `radial_fourier_state_curve(..., full_validation=True)`;
- curves are combined into `OrderedBoundary2D` in stored order.

Do not sort components by current centroid after each step.

## 5.2 Direct forward seam

Use

```text
predict_multicomponent_kress_paired_boundary_response
```

for ordinary objective evaluations.

Do not route an already-owned radial boundary through SDF extraction.

Do not duplicate Kress self/cross kernels.

## 5.3 TD field helper

Implement the non-empty current-domain TD from the validated expression.

At one frequency:

1. build the current multi-component Kress system once;
2. solve one combined RHS batch containing:
   - the 24 physical source points with strength `1e-6`;
   - the 24 paired receiver points used as reciprocal unit sources with strength
     `1`;
3. retain the boundary states;
4. evaluate total fields on the inspection points;
5. pair physical-source field `s` only with reciprocal receiver field `s`;
6. apply the exact production objective scale \(w_f/s_f^2\).

Do not sum over the full 24-by-24 source/receiver matrix.

Do not loop over inspection points with separate solves.

### Memory rule

The inspection-grid receiver operator may be large at the refined raster.

Chunk **receiver-point evaluation only** after the single system/RHS solve.
Use a declared default chunk of `4096` inspection points.

Chunking must not change the field numerically beyond floating-point roundoff.

## 5.4 Empty-background branch

T0 does not use a fake zero-radius or zero-contrast boundary.

For an empty current domain:

```text
predicted scattered field = 0
forward total field = free-space line-source Green field
reciprocal field = free-space unit-source Green field
```

A finite inserted circle is evaluated with the independent circle oracle for
the asymptotic regression.

# 9. T2 / G3 — one finite birth

Use the **production** 121-grid TD region to generate the seed.

Do not use the true B centre or radius.

Test the frozen radius ladder in order.

For each valid candidate compute:

\[
\Delta J_{\rm prod}
=
J_{\rm new}^{64}-J_{\rm old}^{64},
\]

and for candidates with production decrease also compute

\[
\Delta J_{\rm ref}
=
J_{\rm new}^{128}-J_{\rm old}^{128}.
\]

A candidate is numerically accepted only if:

1. \(\Delta J_{\rm prod}<0\);
2. \(\Delta J_{\rm ref}<0\);
3. the decrease exceeds the cross-resolution uncertainty:

\[
\min(-\Delta J_{\rm prod},-\Delta J_{\rm ref})
>
5|\Delta J_{\rm prod}-\Delta J_{\rm ref}|+10^{-12};
\]

4. all geometry/clearance checks pass at both resolutions.

training objective. The refined solve is a numerical qualification, not a
different optimization objective.
