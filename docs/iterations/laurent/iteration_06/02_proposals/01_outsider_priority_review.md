# Outsider priority review — park generic trace compression as the main direction

2026-09-18. Requested by the user: “again, as an outsider, keep chasing or nah”.
Author: Codex, also the implementation owner. This is an outsider-style strategic
assessment, not an independent numerical review. **Recommendation**, not a claim
that the user has adopted a pause or authorized a new experiment.

**Verdict: park the current compression/enrichment sequence as a primary research
agenda. Preserve its verified tools and evidence.** A technically plausible next
correction is not enough reason to allocate another cycle.

## What the evidence actually buys

[LAU-004](../../../../../results/validation/laurent/LAU-004-20260917-closeout/README.md)
shows a valid construction improvement: rank 80→56 on the ellipse and 120→112
on the asymmetric star under matched selection. Its guard/rebuild policy returns
42/42 physically passing outputs. These are real correctness/rank findings.
However, of the 36 nonanchor cases, 24 rebuild. The 48-dimensional primal/adjoint
model rebuilds on every nonanchor candidate. No complete inverse ran and there
is no measured total-time or recovery advantage against the qualified nodal/
compiled path. Full current operator assembly and expensive stability calculations
remain. Correct delivery after recomputation does not establish useful reuse.

The half-dimension threshold is an experiment gate, not a law of useful reduction.
Missing it is not the main reason to park this line. A 56% basis could be valuable
with cheap construction and long reuse. Neither economic condition is established
here. Likewise, the physical residual gate is much stricter than percent-level
noise; noise-aware tolerances could change the result. That possibility requires
an inverse-quality/cost comparison, not relabeling the present failed settings.

The existing cost evidence also argues against assuming this is the dominant
bottleneck. The [Laurent cost record](../../README.md#measured-cost-position--read-this-before-proposing-a-speed-claim)
shows native assembly disadvantaged against nodal Kress on its measured fixtures.
The separate [SPD-008 result](../../../speedup/iteration_08/01_results.md) obtains
full-inverse improvements from geometry work. Those older/local measurements
are not a current whole-pipeline profile, but no evidence identifies the tiny
98/194-dimensional linear solve as the main remaining cost to attack.

## Distinctiveness and opportunity cost

Projection-based parametric model reduction already has established
[interpolation theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.11269), and boundary-integral
shape dependence has established connections to
[reduced models and shape inversion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19853).
These are related precedents, not exact reproductions of our transmission setup.
They rule out treating projection or shape-dependent reduction alone as the
innovation. We have not isolated a Laurent-specific advantage of the present
POD/guard recipe over an analogous nodal construction.

Our stronger candidate question concerns what the inverse can identify. The
[neighbour/calibration experiment](../../../../../results/experiments/laurent_neighbour_20260916/report.md)
already measures target-harmonic error 0.654→0.257→0.118 mm for isolated,
unknown-neighbour additive, and unknown-neighbour coupled settings in its
interaction-specific configuration. This is selected synthetic feasibility,
not established generality or novelty, but it directly changes recovery and
has an explicit mechanism control. It deserves priority consideration over
another rank/rebuild refinement.

## Resolution of the current next-step proposals

| Recommendation | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Continue residual-correction enrichment next | **Reject as current priority** | No established end-to-end cost bottleneck or distinctive inverse benefit; extra corrections can still require full solves |
| Repair the gradient implementation | **Reject** | The full and frozen-model tangents are qualified; no identified formula defect motivates such work |
| Preserve derivative checks, protected spans and guard code | **Accept** | Useful infrastructure and an important accurate-field/wrong-gradient counterexample; no production promotion |
| Launch a wider compression campaign | **Reject** | The predeclared pilot release failed; a larger fixture table would not settle the economic question |
| Study shape/calibration identifiability using the compiled scattering path | **Defer to a focused successor proposal** | More direct recovery evidence, but mechanism/novelty/generalization still need a discriminating question |

Reopen compression only when a concrete application shows substantial cost in
repeated solves/actions with reusable geometry, or when a new construction avoids
full assembly and basis-refresh solves. Its acceptance metric should be total
time/work at matched reconstruction quality, including setup and failures. This
review does not authorize that experiment and imposes no new permission rule.

No numerical code, thresholds, original results, branch or worktree changed
for this review. The prior suggestion of enrichment remains a technical option;
this dated review revises its priority rather than rewriting that history.
