## 5. Acceptance criteria and stopping conditions used by those runs

These are the rules the reference runs actually applied; a comparison that
changes any of them must say so.

**Fixed-topology inner optimizer** (`run_multiradial_fd_inverse`) — central-FD
Levenberg–Marquardt with strict objective decrease, backtracking, geometry
rejection and exact accepted-state rollback. Stop reasons observed:
`loss_change_tolerance`, `relative_step_tolerance`, `no_decreasing_step`,
`infeasible_jacobian`.

**Topology event acceptance** (`topology_controller.py:631`) — a candidate is
accepted only if it decreases the production objective by more than
`acceptance_absolute_margin + acceptance_relative_margin * base_loss`, **and**
the same decrease survives at the refined 128-node resolution within
`cross_resolution_factor`. Among the survivors the lowest production loss wins
(`topology_controller.py:654`). Geometry validity (component clearance,
intersection, feature-radius floor) is checked before scoring.

**Controller stopping** — `recovered` when the relative data error falls to
`relative_error_tolerance`; otherwise `maximum_events`, `maximum_cycles`, or
`topology_stationary` when no candidate is acceptable.

**Challenge qualification** — component count, cross-resolution topology
acceptance, monotone accepted objectives, training and holdout relative L2,
geometry error, and production-versus-refined prediction agreement. `full_pass`
means every declared gate passed at that case's declared tolerances, not that
the error is uniformly small.

## 6. Demonstrated scope at B0

### Implemented capability (verified by reading the code at B0)

- Multi-component ordered Kress/Müller forward with cross-component clearance
  checks (`gpr_bem_kress/multicomponent.py`).
- Immutable `MultiRadialFourierState` geometry with persistent component IDs,
  holding radial **or** Cartesian Fourier components in the same state.
- Automatic topology controller with birth, death, split and merge candidates
  from empty-background and current-domain topological derivatives, a finite
  candidate search, short candidate refinement, and production/refined
  acceptance. Runs in either chart under `chart='radial'|'cartesian'`.
- Two distinct explicit optimizer paths, which must not be conflated:
  the single-component driver uses a full-coefficient FD Jacobian with the phase
  gauge projected out and one re-gauging per accepted step; the topology path
  uses `run_multiradial_fd_inverse(cartesian_gauge=True)`, which solves inside
  the gauge-fixed subspace.
- Validated **single-interface** discrete Kress shape derivative
  (`gpr_bem_kress/shape_derivative.py`), consumed by the material inverses and
  by the implicit-MLP pullback — **not** by the explicit multi-component
  topology path, which is finite-difference only.

### Recorded experimental evidence (read from bundles, not rerun here)

Five automatic cases recover in both charts with identical accepted event
sequences; three challenge cases `full_pass` in both charts. The audit's fresh
Cartesian run reports final relative L2 `1.10e-06` (death), `7.88e-05` (merge),
`4.28e-07` (mixed), `1.62e-07` (repeated-birth), `6.87e-06` (split).

### Independently rerun verification performed for this record

None. No inverse, forward solve or test suite was executed while writing B0.
The only fresh computation was re-hashing the eight audited source files (§1).
Recorded test counts (505 before the audit's edits, 514 after) are **read from
[`tests.log`](../../results/validation/cartesian_fourier/pipeline-audit-20260910/tests.log)**,
not a run performed here.

### Outside the demonstrated scope

Nested holes, touching or intersecting boundaries, multi-material or
per-component unknown permittivity, measurement noise, non-star-shaped
components under the topology gauge, air/ground interfaces, 3-D, global
convergence guarantees, and any neural-owned geometry in the topology loop.

## 7. Representation scope: the distinction that matters most

Two different statements are easy to merge and must not be.

**A claim about the current gauge/optimisation policy — supported.** Under the
Cartesian *topology* policy every component is required to be gauge-fixed in its
own polar angle. That gauge-fixed set is a **linear subspace** of the
coefficient space: dimension `3` for `K ≤ 2` and `2K − 1` above, against the
chart's `4K + 2` coefficients (`solvers/sdf_inverse/curve_updates.py:1399`).
It coincides with the radial chart one band lower. A contour fit that cannot be
gauge-fixed is refused outright — the split case's topology pass records 12 such
refusals. This is a restriction imposed by the policy that currently works, and
it is the reason the two charts match on all eight cases.

**A claim about Cartesian Fourier curves in general — not supported.** Nothing
at B0 shows that a Cartesian Fourier curve cannot represent a non-star-shaped
boundary; the chart plainly can. The un-gauged chart is implemented and was
*measured and rejected on optimisation grounds* in iteration 1 — free parameter
drift along data-invisible directions, degraded quadrature, and stalled shape
progress — not on expressiveness grounds. The iteration-3 record states this
correctly ("It is implemented and refused by policy, not by capability");
downstream summaries that shorten it to "the Cartesian chart is
radial-equivalent" are describing the policy, not the representation.

Both track briefs treat "what does the gauge cost us?" as an **open question**,
not a settled one.

## 8. Known limitations and missing provenance

1. **The baseline source is committed; some of its evidence is not.** Resolved
   on 2026-09-11 for source (`345038a`) and for the bundles' text artifacts
   (`1c5b0cb`). What remains local-only is the audit bundle's `.npz`/`.png`/
   `.mp4` content, excluded by the repository's existing `results/` binary
   policy. A fresh checkout reproduces the baseline's source and every number,
   but not its rasters, figures or videos. No Git tag was created.
2. **No reference bundle records the commit that produced it**, except the two
   single-component runs. The topology manifests record configuration and truth
   but no source revision, so their numbers cannot be attributed to a specific
   revision from the artifacts alone.
3. **The single-component Cartesian bundle's recorded commit `4f0fd6d` is not
   an ancestor of HEAD.** It survives only as a dangling object; the branch
   carries the rewritten `7dae78e` with the same subject line, and the two trees
   differ (8 files). Treat its recorded revision as indicative, not resolvable.
4. **The controller driver records no forward-solve counts.**
   `run_fourier_topology_controller.py` never writes `evaluation_count`, while
   `run_radial_fourier_topology_challenges.py` does (lines 470, 763, 1163). Any
   compute-matched topology comparison needs that instrumentation added first.
5. **Wall-clock numbers in the dated bundles are not controlled.** Concurrency
   for the radial bundles is unrecorded; the Cartesian cases ran eight-way
   concurrent on 24 cores. Compare solve counts, not seconds, unless the
   comparison is run under declared matched conditions.
6. **Extra exploratory result directories exist** under
   `results/inverse/radial_fourier/topology_controller/development-*` and
   `results/inverse/radial_fourier/topology_challenges/challenge-suite-20260909-091144/{ellipse-star,far-circle}`
   and `…-092248/`. They were untracked when B0 was recorded and were committed
   in `1c5b0cb`. Being in git does not promote them: they are
   development/exploratory runs, not curated evidence, and the curated set
   remains the table in §4.
7. **The 2026-09-10 audit changed the split case.** Its sampled Hausdorff error
   improved from `295.85 µm` to `175.21 µm`; the radial reference is `15.60 nm`.
   Use `175.21 µm` as the B0 number. The dated iteration-3 bundle retains the
   older value and should not be edited.
8. **Unit corrections were applied late.** Several nanometre-scale controller
   errors were originally labelled `µm`. Historical measured JSON was not
   edited; where a document and its JSON disagree, the stored metre value wins.
