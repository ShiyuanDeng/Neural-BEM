Where this leaves us

What we can claim, and what is already queued

Supported by the evidence

  • A maintained 2-D, single-boundary, known-contrast inverse: Cartesian Fourier geometry, node-free modal Müller physics, reciprocal sensitivities, a certified spectral update and a damped shape–frequency schedule.
  • On the noiseless TG-002 benchmark it recovers 26/30, the same count as nodal physics, at about one-fifth of the median time.
  • Accuracy exit and agreement damping + reuse keep all 26 and run faster (median 1.55× and 1.13×).
  • The four failures are shared by every physics and update tried. The Aphex runs end on the resolution gate, and relaxing that gate alone did not recover them; the hook sits in a wrong basin.

Open threads already in the record

  • Policy screen PS-001: 13 registered recipes, committed in 71087669; the referenced plan file is missing and no screen has run.
  • Modal resolution response RP-001 · modal_response: implemented, unqualified.
  • Full TG-002 rerun on the current code, since the AC-001 kernel change has had only component tests.
  • Hook basin PX-001 and blind start transfer FM-006: proposed only.
  • GauGal hybrid GP-001: proposed, awaiting approval.
Not supported yet: a global convergence guarantee, robustness to noisy data, arbitrary topology recovery, end-to-end sample-free certification, recovery by neural-SDF optimization, or superiority over GauGal.
