# Implicit-initialization inverse comparisons

Four checked cases share one pipeline: the same paired ring measurements, the
same ordered Method-B geometry, the same normalized complex objective, the same
bounded parameter finite differences, and the same damped Gauss--Newton policy.
Only the initial implicit representation, the analytic target, and the selected
forward solver change. No observation is ever generated by a solver under test.

The `mlp-` directories are a separate, progress-gated path. Current runs use a
canonical ordered contour as the accepted optimization geometry and a full MLP
as its audited signed-distance representation; they share the measurements and
forward backends but not the parametric optimizer or its accuracy gates. Older
dated bundles below predate that state split and are retained as historical
evidence. See
[the section below](#alternating-mlp-sdf-inverse).

Both branches recovered the center to about `3e-9 m` and the mean radius to
`4.6e-6 m` (MOD) and `9.5e-8 m` (Kress). The observation oracle was gated

The star moves the accuracy floor from the forward solver to the geometry.
Kress' final training error is on the same geometry-controlled order as its

arc-length Fourier representation is material rather than its quadrature; MOD remains limited by its own
discretisation. That is why the Kress holdout gate is `1e-3` here and `1e-6`
for the circle. See
[`docs/solver_neutral_inverse.md`](inverse_development_through_2026-09-06.md) for
the measured bandwidth, frequency-sensitivity, and conditioning evidence
behind the star's configuration.

## Alternating MLP-SDF inverse

These bundles come from
[`run_mlp_sdf_inverse_comparison.py`](../../run_mlp_sdf_inverse_comparison.py),
not the parametric driver. In the current implementation, the accepted state is
a canonical gauge-fixed radial Fourier curve. Finite-difference and line-search
trials increment that state and call MOD or Kress directly on its curve; they do
not extract an SDF. A selected data-decreasing candidate is densely validated
with identical nodes before it is re-distanced into every MLP weight. The extracted MLP
contour is then checked as a representation audit, but it neither replaces the
canonical state nor causes another BEM solve. **These runs carry a progress gate only** -- at least
one accepted cycle with a strict training-loss decrease -- and none of the
parametric driver's accuracy gates. Read them as reconstruction experiments,
not certified results. The historical local-normal bundles below are failed
diagnostics; the latest radial-state bundle recovers the canonical target but
still misses the separate MLP-representation convergence gate.

Training now uses cumulative low-to-high frequency continuation by default.
For the standard three-frequency K5 case this means 0.5 GHz/K3,
0.5+1.5 GHz/K5, then 0.5+1.5+2.5 GHz/K5. `--joint-full-band` and
`--progressive-mode-continuation` remain explicit ablations. The radial
spectral tail is always stored and the canonical global-K tail is constrained
to roundoff by the state itself. Trust-region
damping is first bracketed and then refined in log damping so a decade jump
does not leave a valid step far inside its displacement allowance.
The default is now six backtrack reductions after the un-backtracked
candidate, so the line search reaches `1/64` scale. The former five-reduction
limit omitted the sixth reduction needed to clear the dense-validation barrier
observed in the progressive-mode run. A
stationary direct curve whose MLP still fails the convergence gates now stops
as `representation_limited_stationary`, without a false convergence claim or
repeating the remaining outer budget. A stationary iteration that also rejects
larger candidates in dense validation instead stops as
`geometry_limited_stationary`, and a backtracked accept retains its productive
damping. The displacement trust-radius default
is now `2 mm`: the joint diagnostic's first seven accepted steps saturated its
former `4 mm` cap before the parameterization collapsed.

The tabulated dated bundles precede direct canonical probes, MLP audit-only
projection, frequency continuation, and refined trust-region damping. They are
retained as failure evidence and must not be read as validation of the current
path; the later canonical diagnostics are reported below.

In this historical table, "Exhausted" is
`no_decreasing_redistanced_step`: the old implementation found no damping
trial, backtrack, and re-distancing cycle with a further decrease. Current runs
instead distinguish canonical modal stationarity, topology, optional
spectral-tail, and MLP-representation limits explicitly. The inner/outer
Eikonal tolerances are also ordered so a successful inner fit cannot make the
outer convergence gate unreachable.

The completed pre-canonical
[`mlp-fixed-ellipse-to-star-kress-20260903/`](../../results/legacy/inverse/mlp_feedback/mlp-fixed-ellipse-to-star-kress-20260903)
run is the direct motivation for the current state split. It accepted 25
updates before `spectral_tail_growth_limit`; training relative L2 improved only
`1.194 -> 1.039`, holdout worsened `1.003 -> 1.043`, and maximum boundary error
worsened `4.203e-2 -> 4.667e-2 m`. Its MLP extraction drift remained a
non-vanishing fraction of late modal steps, while the hard 0.2 mm tail allowance
rejected every remaining data-decreasing candidate. Those numbers diagnose the
superseded loop; they are not results for the current canonical continuation.

The completed canonical-state diagnostic
[`mlp-canonical-ellipse-to-star-kress-20260903/`](../../results/legacy/inverse/normal_updates/mlp-canonical-ellipse-to-star-kress-20260903)
did not converge either. It accepted 15 updates, and all three stages stopped
on `no_acceptable_mlp_distillation`. Full-band loss changed
`2.223592919 -> 2.157108810`, training relative L2
`1.194114882 -> 1.031121445`, holdout relative L2
`1.003360055 -> 0.942955819`, and canonical maximum boundary error
`42.0309 -> 27.8343 mm`. Its final representation drift was `0.497114 mm` and
the representation response differed from the canonical response by
`0.00406356` relative.

That partial geometric improvement was not lobe recovery. The cumulative
low-frequency first stage drove translation and shrank mean radius from
`50.982` to `40.345 mm`; the final `k=5` component had the wrong phase and its
vector error worsened from `12.480` to `14.446 mm`. The measured failure led
to the full-band progressive-mode experiment described below. The representation
acceptance gate is now a `1.5 mm` safety cap, while the independent `0.2 mm`
geometry-convergence tolerance still gates convergence. Rejection metrics also
separate dense-validation, MLP-fit, representation-extraction, and drift
failures from genuinely infeasible forward trials.

The canonical state split did remove the original forward bottleneck. Inverse
time fell from `948.326` to `288.945 s`; the latter comprised `28.375 s` of
direct forwards (`26.744 s` BEM and `1.007 s` curve updates), `174.511 s` of
neural re-distancing, and `85.537 s` of geometry/representation audits.
Boundary extraction is no longer repeated or charged inside every modal
forward; neural fitting and selected-candidate auditing now dominate.

## Reading a bundle

Every checked low-dimensional directory contains `metrics.json`, `summary.md`,
one trajectory CSV and compressed response NPZ per solver, and
`convergence.png`. The current MLP driver writes `metrics.json`, `summary.md`,
one trajectory CSV, response NPZ, and model checkpoint per requested solver;
it does not synthesize `convergence.png` during the long solve. MLP bundles
store the canonical final curve/response and the extracted MLP representation
curve/response separately, including their drift and relative response
discrepancy. Continuation stage, active frequencies, and modal ceiling appear
on every trajectory row. New rows also retain the accepted backtrack count,
raw trust-region versus accepted-step predicted relative decrease,
arc-length-refit RMS/maximum displacement, and speed ratio before/after the
refit. The same values are emitted during the live run, making another
parameterization collapse visible before post-processing. Timing is likewise separated into direct objective
forwards, neural re-distancing, and selected-candidate geometry/MLP audits;
boundary extraction is not hidden inside every modal forward.

`contour_evolution.mp4` is optional post-processing, rendered from an existing
bundle by [`run_sdf_inverse_contour_video.py`](../../run_sdf_inverse_contour_video.py):

```bash
python run_sdf_inverse_contour_video.py \
  results/inverse_solver_comparison/wrong-star-nystrom-20260903
```

It animates the stored contour trajectories of the selected solvers against
the exact target. Parametric bundles store accepted iterates only. Continuation
MLP bundles additionally store each new stage's iteration-zero baseline: its
geometry duplicates the preceding accepted contour. Full-band
progressive-mode losses remain comparable across stages. The renderer
conservatively separates every stage segment; that separation is essential for
frequency continuation, whose unlike objectives must not be joined. The joint
default has only one stage. Frames between stored contours are labelled visual
interpolation and change no reported number.

In all four cases the initial family contains the target exactly -- the star
family at the right five parameters, the ellipse family at equal semi-axes,
the random-feature family at zero output weights. These bundles measure
whether the pipeline drives an implicit field onto a representable target and
how forward-solver error biases the recovered parameters; they are not
evidence about shape discovery or model misspecification. See the scope
section of
[`docs/solver_neutral_inverse.md`](inverse_development_through_2026-09-06.md).

Every checked bundle passes all of its acceptance gates: 25 for each circle
case and 30 for the star, which adds lobe-depth, lobe-phase, and
oracle-self-convergence gates. The two non-SDF circle cases additionally gate
that the initial zero contour is materially non-circular and that the field
gradient is materially non-unit. Raw implicit residual magnitude is retained
only as a diagnostic because it changes under field rescaling; the comparable
geometric residual is `|F| / ||grad F||`.

The random-feature case is a small topology-constrained neural implicit. Its
seeded tanh features are fixed, while center, base radius, and four output
weights are optimized. Bounds guarantee one positive star-shaped radial zero
contour. It is not a claim that an arbitrary randomly initialized SIREN is
valid: a seeded unconstrained MLP is covered by an expected-failure regression
and is rejected during contour validation before either forward solver runs.

That run completed with 82 accepted canonical updates but did not converge:
training relative L2 fell from `1.1924` to `0.4482`, holdout relative L2 ended
at `0.7568`, and maximum boundary error fell from `42.03` to `17.32 mm`.
The trace exposed two numerical blockers rather than a BEM failure.  Of its
198 full-validation rejections, replayed candidates were regular simple curves
that failed only because a 1024-point finite-difference audit under-resolved
the bandwidth-48 third derivative; those candidates pass at 2048 points.  It
then stopped at `no_acceptable_mlp_distillation` with the canonical loss still
descending and the MLP drift at `1.377 mm`, just below its `1.5 mm` cap.

The current implementation therefore uses a separate bandwidth-aware
derivative grid, keeps the 1024-point topology grid, applies denser boundary
and normal-offset supervision, gives warm-started re-distancing a stronger
zero-set anchor with no repeated distance-only warmup, and may retain the
previous audited MLP for one bounded canonical descent while it remains inside
the same drift cap. The subsequent resolved acquisition run is stored in
[`mlp-resolved-k5-ellipse-to-star-kress-20260904/`](../../results/legacy/inverse/normal_updates/mlp-resolved-k5-ellipse-to-star-kress-20260904).
It also did not converge. Its 56 accepted updates changed training relative L2
`1.19325 -> 0.79976`, holdout relative L2 `1.13855 -> 1.07342`, and maximum
boundary error `42.22 -> 46.92 mm`. The MLP remained faithful (`0.643 mm`
final drift), all 56 re-distance/extraction audits succeeded, and no candidate
failed full geometry validation. The wrong result is therefore genuinely the
canonical optimizer's path, not an extraction or BEM failure.

That trace reveals two remaining structural defects. Repeated local normal
updates ceased to be star-shaped and grew the radial tail above `k=5` from
`0.723` to `6.258 mm` RMS, so the controls did not define a global K5 shape
space. Separately, a direction audit found the initial joint
0.5/1.5/2.5 GHz K5 step target-misaligned, while 0.5 GHz/K3 is strongly
target-aligned. The old frequency continuation was not a valid counterexample:
it forced K1 at 0.5 GHz and could not correct the ellipse's dominant K2 term.

The current implementation holds a gauge-fixed radial Fourier state through
all stages and defaults to 0.5 GHz/K3 -> 0.5+1.5 GHz/K5 -> full-band/K5. The
150-update allocation is 19/20/111, with unused warm-stage capacity carried to
the final stage. The completed validating command was:
