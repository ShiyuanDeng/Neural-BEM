Quick meeting findings — 16 September 2026

Five previously failed topology scenes recovered from their original geometric starts. All twelve scenes now have saved endpoints passing the original gates and 256/512-node numerical checks. No target component count supplied.

Working recipe: topology sensitivities at 500 + 750 MHz, circle seeds padded to mode 3 so they can deform, initial states padded to mode 3, existing bandwidth promotion, and geometry guards at the continuation resolutions. True analytic Jacobians throughout. Shape refinement uses 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.75, 2 GHz. The 1.5 and 2.5 GHz responses are evaluation-only.

The five previous failures have maximum matched boundary errors of 0.0523, 0.0523, 0.0524, 0.0467, and 0.00167 mm respectively. Largest held-out relative field error among them: 0.1032%.

Five circular positive controls already pass after the new topology phase. Merge and central ellipse/star received the six-frequency shape refinement. This is exploratory evidence with additional data and differing budgets, not a frozen equal-budget benchmark.

The production patch is a small opt-in continuation-resolution geometry guard. It prevents the recorded case where 64/128-node geometry passes but the 512-node handoff violates clearance. Experimental policy changes live in experiments/topology_playground_20260916.

Frequency nuance: more iterations with the old four frequencies did not free the archived two-star minimum. Adding 1.75 + 2 GHz did. Along the sampled feasible path toward the recovered shape, the old loss rises about 23x, while the augmented loss decreases. Jacobian condition number improves from 182.5 to 11.84. A fresh better topology start also succeeds with the original four-frequency continuation.

Not every frequency/resolution change helps: 250 + 500 MHz left a poor ellipse/star shape; 500 + 750 MHz with rigid circle seeds still gave three objects. 128/256-node shape fitting passed for three shapes (4.7 versus 16.4 minutes), but failed the strict intermediate numerical check for two stars. 192/384 passed the two-star case in 5.5 minutes. All quoted final endpoint checks still use 256/512. Timings are observed exploratory wall times, not controlled performance benchmarks.

Three-material prototype: sand epsr=6, plastic epsr=3, ideal PEC metal (TMz Ez=0). An empty-start circular insertion search infers count and plastic/metal labels, then jointly fits centers/radii with a coupled analytic Jacobian. The data are generated by an independent coupled BEM solver. Material properties and the circle family are supplied; target count, labels, and individual object boxes are not.

With the project acquisition geometry, both noise seeds recover two correctly labelled circles at 1% and 5% noise. Maximum center errors across those seeds: 0.0429 mm at 1%, 0.212 mm at 5%. Maximum held-out errors: 0.440% and 2.211%. Separate one-metal, three-object, and swapped-label controls also succeeded. This is a circular PEC prototype, not finite-conductivity metal or a general material-aware topology controller.

Validation: 67 tests passed, 1 skipped (CUDA unavailable). Includes analytic Jacobian checks against independent BEM geometry perturbations, reciprocity, series/grid convergence, and PEC boundary conditions. git diff --check passed.
